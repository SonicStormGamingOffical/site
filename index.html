Features

💻

One live tab

Only the tab you're using stays loaded. Everything else is suspended instantly, so the browser barely touches your CPU and memory.

⏳

Instant switching

Switch to a suspended tab and it reloads right away. Your session, history and logged-in state are preserved per profile.

👥

Profiles

Create separate profiles for work, personal, school — each with its own cookies, sessions and tabs. Add and delete them freely.

🔖

Storage saver

A capped disk cache plus automatic cache sweeps keep the installation small. Deep-clean removes all browsing data whenever you want.

📖

Bookmarks

A built-in bookmark bar. Save a page with one click and open it from the bar anytime. Export or import your bookmarks as an HTML file.

🧠

Extensions + AdGuard

The AdGuard ad blocker is included out of the box, and you can load more extensions (.crx or unpacked folders) from Settings.

How it works

  1. Open a tab. That tab is the only one fully loaded.
  2. Switch away. The old tab is suspended and its page is unloaded, freeing the renderer's CPU and memory.
  3. Come back. The tab reloads exactly where you left it.

No analytics, no ad trackers built in. Your browsing stays on your machine.
